Cloud Before Code — Lesson 6: Who Can Change the Menu?

Michael Rovas  ·  6271-ITAI-3371 Cloud Config & Resources AI  ·  Instructor: Karthik Rajan  ·  Nov 17, 2026

The situation LunchRush is a small food-ordering business. Last night, a customer was charged the wrong price. Someone changed it at 11 p.m. — no one knows who. Jordan asks: "Does everyone on the team have access to everything?"

Step 1 — Before the vocabulary: your first thought

Think of a place with different access levels (a hospital, office, school). What question would you ask to decide what access a person should have?

Step 2 — LunchRush access policy

  • Jordan Owner — can view and change anything.
  • Sam Menu manager — can edit menu items and photos. Cannot access financial records or change prices.
  • Riley Delivery coordinator — can view orders and update delivery status. Cannot change menu or financial data.
  • Alex Tech helper — can view and change technical configuration. Does not need customer payment data during normal operations.

▶ Apply least privilege: give each person only the access they need to do their job.

Step 3 — Permissions table

R = Read (view only) W = Write (view + change) — = No access
